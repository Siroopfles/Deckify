    - `PowerGovernor.evaluate_now()` computes:

    - `PowerGovernor.wake_for_playback()` clears `_eco_sleeping = False` and `_paused_since = None`, starts engine via `await plugin.start_librespot()` if not running, polls `get_devices` (up to `4 * 0.3s`) for a Connect device if needed, and emits `eco_status_changed`.
