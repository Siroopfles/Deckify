<objective>
Restore and polish the Deckify in-game floating HUD overlay across gameplay and SteamOS GamepadUI navigation.

### User Story
**As a** Steam Deck gamer listening to Spotify in the background,
**I want to** see a smooth, non-intrusive floating HUD with track artwork, title, artist, and play/pause status in my preferred corner,
**so that** I can track music playback without leaving my game, with configurable auto-hide, pause grace fade-out, optional progress bar, and instant QAM toggle controls.

Purpose: Fix state desynchronization in `DeckifyOverlayWrapper` by polling `track`, `is_playing`, `position_ms`, `duration_ms`, and `settings` concurrently; add configurable 6-second auto-hide (`overlay_autohide`) and 3.5-second pause grace period; add optional 2px progress line (`overlay_show_progress`); integrate direct controls into the Quick Access Menu; and ensure zero touch/click interception over games via strict `pointerEvents: "none"`.
Output: Updated `py_modules/common.py`, `main.py`, `src/types.ts`, `tests/test_e2e_requirements.py`, `src/components/DeckifyOverlay.tsx`, new `src/components/DeckifyOverlay.test.tsx`, updated `src/index.tsx`, and updated `src/components/tabs/SettingsTab.tsx`.
