<objective>
Migrate Spotify track liking (`toggle_like_track`) and batch liked-status checks (`check_tracks_liked`) from deprecated `/v1/me/tracks` endpoints to `/v1/me/library` and `/v1/me/library/contains` (max 40 URIs), and add Official Spotify Client CDP `LibraryAPI` 403 fallbacks for `toggle_like_track` and `get_liked_tracks`.

Purpose: Eliminate silent HTTP 403 Forbidden errors on Development Mode Spotify apps while preserving resilient fallback behavior for `spotify_client` users.
Output: Updated `py_modules/engine.py`, `py_modules/rpc_playback.py`, `py_modules/rpc_library.py`, updated `tests/test_features.py` & `tests/test_final_coverage.py`, and new `tests/test_phase13.py`.
</objective>
