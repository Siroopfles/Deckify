## Component Layout & Interaction Contract (`SettingsTab.tsx`)

### 1. 7 Preset Pills (Two Horizontal Rows)
Inside the `"Equalizer & Audio Presets"` card (`maxWidth: 640`):
- **Header row:** `<h3 ...><FaSlidersH /> Equalizer & Audio Presets</h3>` with right-aligned status indicator:
  - When `eq_preset === "flat"`: `Bypass (0.0 dB)` (`opacity: 0.6`)
  - When `eq_preset !== "flat"`: `Preamp: {preampDb.toFixed(1)} dB` (`color: "#1db954"`, where `preampDb = -Math.max(0, ...bands)`)
- **Row 1 (`<Focusable flow-children="horizontal" style={{ display: "flex", gap: 8, marginBottom: 8 }}>`):**
  1. `flat` — `Flat`
  2. `bass` — `Bass Boost`
  3. `vocal` — `Vocal Clarity`
  4. `gaming` — `Gaming Balance`
- **Row 2 (`<Focusable flow-children="horizontal" style={{ display: "flex", gap: 8, marginBottom: 12 }}>`):**
  5. `deck_speakers` — `Deck Speakers`
  6. `late_night` — `Late Night`
  7. `custom` — `Custom`

### 2. Always-Visible 10-Band ISO `SliderField` Controls (`D-01`, `D-02`)
Rendered directly below Row 2 inside `<Focusable flow-children="column" style={{ display: "flex", flexDirection: "column", gap: 4 }}>`:
- Bands (index `0`–`9`):
  - `0`: `31 Hz`
  - `1`: `62 Hz`
  - `2`: `125 Hz`
  - `3`: `250 Hz`
  - `4`: `500 Hz`
  - `5`: `1 kHz`
  - `6`: `2 kHz`
  - `7`: `4 kHz`
  - `8`: `8 kHz`
  - `9`: `16 kHz`
- Props on every `SliderField`:
  - `min={-12}`
  - `max={12}`
  - `step={0.5}`
  - `showValue`
  - `value={localBands[idx]}`
  - `onChange={(val) => handleBandChange(idx, val)}`

### 3. Preset-to-Custom Auto-Fork & Live Update Flow (`D-02`, `D-07`)
- **Clicking a preset pill (`flat`–`late_night`):**
  1. Optimistically snaps `localEqPreset` to `eq.id` and `localBands` to that preset's 10-band curve.
  2. Calls `backendSetSetting("eq_preset", eq.id)` and `showToast("Audio preset: " + eq.label)`.
- **Clicking `Custom` pill:**
  1. Optimistically sets `localEqPreset` to `"custom"` and `localBands` to `localCustomBands` (persisted `eq_custom_bands`).
  2. Calls `backendSetSetting("eq_preset", "custom")` and `showToast("Audio preset: Custom")`.
- **Adjusting any `SliderField` (`handleBandChange`):**
  1. Rounds/clamps value to `[-12.0, 12.0]` in `0.5` steps.
  2. Immediately updates `localBands`, sets `localCustomBands` to the new 10-band array, and forks `localEqPreset` to `"custom"`.
  3. Calls `backendSetEqBands(nextBands)` (backend `EqualizerManager` coalesces `pw-cli set-param` to `~75ms` and debounces `settings.json` persistence by `500ms`).
- **External `settings_changed` / `eq_preset_changed` events:**
  - Sync `localEqPreset` and `localBands` from backend settings when not actively dragging, keeping UI consistent across QAM/modal reloads.

## UI Considerations

Applicable state considerations resolved: 6 covered, 0 backstop, 0 unresolved.

| Category | Element(s) | Status | Resolution / Reason |
|----------|------------|--------|---------------------|
| Spatial D-pad navigation | 7 EQ preset pills (`Flat`..`Custom`) | ✅ covered | Preset pills are split into two `<Focusable flow-children="horizontal">` rows (4 pills in Row 1, 3 pills in Row 2) with `flex: 1` and `gap: 8` — never `flow-children="row"`, preventing SteamOS GamepadUI vertical focus jumps. |
| 10-band ISO layout & live drag | 10 `SliderField` controls (`31 Hz`–`16 kHz`) | ✅ covered | All 10 `SliderField` bands (`min={-12}`, `max={12}`, `step={0.5}`, `showValue`) remain always visible in a `<Focusable flow-children="column">` stack below the preset rows so D-pad Up/Down steps cleanly between bands and Left/Right adjusts gain in `0.5 dB` increments with `~75ms` backend `pw-cli` coalescing. |
| Preset-to-custom auto-fork | Preset pills ↔ 10 `SliderField` sliders | ✅ covered | Selecting a preset snaps all 10 sliders to that preset's curve; moving any slider immediately forks `eq_preset` to `"custom"` and updates `eq_custom_bands`, while clicking `Custom` restores the saved custom curve. |
| Compact QAM constraint (D-08) | Quick Access Menu (`src/index.tsx`) | ✅ covered | QAM remains untouched with zero EQ controls, preset pills, or EQ badges; all equalizer controls live exclusively in `SettingsTab.tsx`. |
| Empty / bypass state | `Flat` preset active (`0.0 dB` all bands) | ✅ covered | Selecting `Flat` snaps all 10 sliders to `0.0 dB`, displays `Bypass (0.0 dB)` in the card header, and tears down `pipewire -c` for true zero-DSP bypass while keeping sliders visible and ready to fork on adjustment. |
| Error / crash fallback state | `pipewire -c` startup or runtime failure | ✅ covered | If `EqualizerManager` fails to start or exits unexpectedly, `librespot` falls back directly to the hardware sink and `SettingsTab.tsx` surfaces the documented error toast (`Equalizer engine unavailable — routed directly to hardware output`). |
