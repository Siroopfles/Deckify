<objective>
Close UAT gaps G-27-9 and G-27-10: eliminate the mid-track audio stutter and app hang when dropping a reordered track or removing/clearing tracks in QueueTab.

Purpose: Calling `PUT /v1/me/player/play` (`_mutate_queue_via_uri_slice`) while a track is actively playing restarts the audio stream at a slightly stale `position_ms`, causing an audible stutter on drop/remove. Resolving `uid`/`target_uid` in CDP, deferring non-CDP/context-track mutations until natural track transition, fixing `handleReorderQueueTrack` to pass `preGrabTracks`, and shifting focus on inline `✕` unmount eliminates both stutters and focus loss.
Output: Updated CDP bridge, playback RPCs, `_poll_playback` transition hook, `DeckifyExpandedModal.tsx`, `QueueTab.tsx`, and unit tests.
</objective>
