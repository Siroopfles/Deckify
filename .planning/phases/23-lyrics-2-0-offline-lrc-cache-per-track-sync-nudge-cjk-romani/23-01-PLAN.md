<objective>
Implement the Phase 23 backend foundation for Lyrics 2.0 (`LYRICS-01`): human-readable `.lrc` disk caching under `~/.cache/deckify/lyrics/`, dedicated atomic per-track sync offset persistence in `~/.config/deckify/lyrics_offsets.json`, RPC bindings (`get_lyrics` with `track_id`/`force_refresh`, `get_track_lyrics_offset`, `set_track_lyrics_offset`, `clear_lyrics_cache`), and romanization setting persistence in `common.py` / `main.py`.

Purpose: Enables `0ms` repeat/offline lyrics loads and persistent per-track sync nudging without mutating `.lrc` timestamps or bloating `settings.json`.
Output: `py_modules/lyrics_offsets.py`, updated `py_modules/spotify.py`, `py_modules/rpc_library.py`, `py_modules/common.py`, `main.py`, and `tests/test_lyrics_2.py`.
