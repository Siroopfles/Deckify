- **Cross-Phase Wiring:** All 10 requirements are wired across backend modules (`engine.py`, `volume.py`, `system.py`, `equalizer.py`, `dock.py`, `resilience.py`, `mpris.py`, `common.py`, `main.py`) and frontend (`SettingsTab.tsx`, `usePlaybackStatus.ts`, `index.tsx`, `DeckifyExpandedModal.tsx`). 0 orphaned exports or broken links.
- **Key Cross-Phase Interactions Verified:**
  1. **Phase 13 (`LIB-02`) → Phases 14/15/16:** `resolve_spotify_sink_input_ids()` strips `steamwebhelper` and `Chromium` blocks before PID/allowlist matching across `VolumeFader`, `EqualizerManager`, and `DockAudioSwitcher`.
  2. **Phase 14 (`SLEEP-01`) → Phase 18 (`MPRIS-01`):** `MprisBridge` checks `VolumeFader._sleep_locked` before applying Bluetooth/AVRCP volume writes, and preserves `_duck_reasons` when recalculating ducked sink volume.

  4. **Phase 17 (`RESIL-01`, `RESIL-02`) ↔ Phase 18 (`MPRIS-01`):** `LibrespotEventServer.process_payload()` forwards events to `MprisBridge.on_onevent()`, and `MprisBridge.sync_from_plugin()` uses `resilience.normalize_spotify_id()` to guard against stale metadata during the 400ms enrichment window.
